Questions

Before you call

Where do you deliver?

Delivery is free within Krynica-Zdrój. If you are staying outside the town, ask on the phone whether we can reach you.

When do you take orders?

Daily from 12:00 to 22:00. Hours can differ on public holidays; we announce them on Facebook.

How long does delivery take?

It depends on the time of day and the size of the order. We will give you an estimate when you order.

How can I pay?

You agree the payment method with us when you place the order.

Can I order for a set time?

Yes. Call ahead and tell us the time. For larger orders, such as several pans for four, please call well in advance.
